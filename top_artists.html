Top Artists

Top 50 artist leaderboard generated from weekdata.js. This version follows the attached certification page logic: it picks the same weekly field order, detects Album/EP vs Single/Track, then converts raw sales using ×1,750 for singles/tracks and ×500 for albums/EPs.

Hard limit: the ranked list is cut to exactly 50 before rendering. Search only filters inside those 50. Sales That Year = weekly raw chart value × the correct multiplier for each entry. Sales To Date = cumulative artist total up to the selected year.
